            How the forecast is made

            1. Collect. A scheduled job pulls hourly pollutant readings for every tracked city from Open-Meteo every few hours and saves them to this site's data files.
            2. Combine. Those readings join official daily AQI from CPCB (2022–2025) and a Kaggle history for five metros (from 2018).
            3. Learn. A gradient-boosted model learns how AQI moves from the last three weeks, the season and the crop-burning period, once per forecast day.
            4. Publish. The forecast and its likely range are written to the files this page reads, and the site redeploys.

            How accurate is it?

            Hawa is a student project, not an official advisory. AQI here follows the CPCB National AQI. For health decisions, check the CPCB or your local authority.
